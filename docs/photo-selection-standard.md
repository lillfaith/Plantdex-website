# PlantDex photo selection standard (DRAFT v0, from #62-66; unvalidated until the #77-81 blind test)

For a person or model choosing iNaturalist photographs per card. Based on five species: treat as hypotheses.

**Hard gates (bytes and facts only):** CC0 on the individual photo; observation taxon is the species; original long
edge >= 1200px; the feature the role needs is visible in full. Nothing else rejects a photo outright.

**Do not reject for:** hands or gloves, grass or habitat behind the plant, a landscape-shaped frame, a stalk that
leaves the frame (crop it), a small plant in a wide scene (crop it), airy or sparse branching.

**Main:** one plant or branch that reads as this species; stems and leaves connect to the flowers; width usable for the
art window after cropping; extraction does not cut through anatomy.
**Pixel:** one identifying feature, forward-facing, angular silhouette, natural contrast, anatomy connected. It may be a
bud, seed pod or fruit rather than a flower when that is more diagnostic. Must survive the real 24x24 reduction.
**Tall:** one upright connected unit (it may sit inside a landscape photo), slender enough for the oval, leaves and
flowers both visible, foreground branch complete. Judge the unit, not the whole frame.

**Across the three roles:** never recommend one photograph for two roles; make the pixel source a different organ from
the main when the species has one; keep at least one alternative per role.

**Record for every pick:** photo id, observation id, licence check date, role, proposed crop, mask check, native 24px
check, card-fit check, reason selected, reason rejected.

**Needs a person:** is the feature diagnostic; is it elegant; can the hand be removed without losing anatomy.
**Never:** regenerate or invent botany; change the template, icons or gradient; promote anything unfinished.
