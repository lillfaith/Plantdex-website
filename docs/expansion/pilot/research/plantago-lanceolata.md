## (c) Safety caution

Question: What cautions apply to narrowleaf plantain, and does 'Seeds swell; drink plenty of water' apply to this species?

- **Leaf: no hazard identified** ([ema-pl]): The EMA assessment report on the leaf (read in excerpt) recorded traditional use and constituents; no hazard statement for the leaf was found in the excerpt read.
- **Seed swelling / obstruction applies to psyllium** ([ema-psyllium], [drugs-plantago]): Swelling and throat-obstruction warnings are documented for psyllium seed (P. afra/P. indica/P. ovata) products. NOT FOUND for P. lanceolata seed or husk.
- **Allergy** ([drugs-plantago]): Plantago pollen contains allergens and psyllium has caused anaphylaxis; the genus-level pollen statement is not specific to this species.

**Where sources disagree / what is unresolved:** No disagreement; a gap. The draft's seed-swelling caution is borrowed from psyllium and is not sourced for P. lanceolata.

**Draft card warning:** "Seeds swell; drink plenty of water." - UNSUPPORTED AS WRITTEN for this species (NOT FOUND for P. lanceolata). It is a sound caution for psyllium products, and the card lists 'Seed Fiber' as a preparation and 'Seed' as a usable part, neither of which is sourced for P. lanceolata. As written it also reads as preparation advice (how to take seeds safely).

**Draft guide caution:** None in the draft (null).

**Proposed site caution:** PROPOSED DRAFT FOR REVIEW, NOT FINAL WORDING: 'Seeds of some plantain species (psyllium) swell in liquid and can block the throat if swallowed without plenty of water. This has not been studied for narrowleaf plantain. Plantdex does not recommend eating the seeds or any wild plant.' Alternative: drop the seed lines from this card until they are sourced.

**Open safety questions**

- Remove 'Seed Fiber' and 'Seed' from the draft, or source them for P. lanceolata?
- Does the owner want an allergy line (pollen)? Genus-level only.
- Is 'Bite soothing' appropriate even as a traditional-use phrase when snake bites are mentioned in the source? Recommend rewording or omitting.
