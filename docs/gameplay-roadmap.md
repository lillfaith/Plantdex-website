# Gameplay roadmap

Items here are **proposals until the owner approves them**. Nothing below is built.

## Boss Form and the expanded plant profile — PROPOSED, audit complete, awaiting approval

Every collectible plant should eventually have a fourth, final **Boss Form** sprite that
represents complete mastery, and one comprehensive **expanded plant profile** that becomes
part of the mastery journey. Neither may be earned by merely learning the card or opening
the profile. Full audit and proposal: [`boss-form-and-expanded-profile.md`](./boss-form-and-expanded-profile.md).

Standing constraints carried over from the owner: no retroactive Boss Forms, existing
achievements and XP safeguards preserved, one profile per plant with no duplicate
information systems, safety-critical identification information reachable without
discovery, and the current onboarding work kept separate.
