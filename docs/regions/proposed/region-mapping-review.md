# Independent review of the EPA Level III to PlantDex-region mapping (PROPOSAL, unreviewed)

Researched 2026-11-24/25. Replaces the assumptions behind `region-mapping.csv` (v1, now **superseded**). Output: `region-mapping-v2.csv`
(85 rows) and `region-legend.csv`. Generated by `scripts/regions/make_mapping_v2.py` from the reviewed table `scripts/regions/mapping_v2_table.py`.
Nothing here is imported into the app.

## 1. What is official and what is PlantDex's

| | Official | PlantDex's own |
|---|---|---|
| The 85 Level III polygons, their numbers and names, their Level II/I groups | **EPA** (`us_eco_l3`, 2013 metadata; see `licence-and-provenance.md`) | |
| Which of the ten regions a unit belongs to | | **Nothing recovered.** The owner's definition names the ten regions and lists them per card; it gives no boundary (`region-taxonomy.md` s3) |
| Every row of `region-mapping-v2.csv` | | **My proposal** |

EPA's own Level I groups are not PlantDex regions and the CSV does not follow them. The EPA units are *environmental* units ("areas of general
similarity in ecosystems"), not floristic ones, and EPA's metadata describes the lines as zones of gradual transition.

## 2. How independent this review is, and where it is not

The first mapping (v1) was made against **assumed region names**, so its agreement with itself proved nothing, and agreement with an assumed
reference is not botanical evidence. The v2 review re-read every row against the **recovered** names and measured two things the v1 never did.
It is still one reviewer (me) with one body of knowledge, so it is **not** a botanical validation. Four lines of evidence, in descending strength:

1. **Geometry (measured).** For each unit, the share of its outline that touches a unit carried in a different region
   (`outline_shared_with_other_regions`, from 1e-4 degree shared edges). This is how I found that the alternative I had written for several units was
   not the alternative the map says: unit 71 (Interior Plateau) shares 50% of its outline with MW units and I had listed only AP; unit 61 (Erie
   Drift Plain) shares 56% with AP units; 83 shares 27% with NF units. Those alternatives were added. It also found three rows naming an alternative that
   does not border the unit at all (36, 80, and 23, which still stands and is flagged, `alternative_borders_unit = NO`).
2. **The wording of the owner's region names** (`basis = NAME`; recovered fact): "Appalachian-Ozark Highlands" puts the Ozarks and Ouachitas with the Appalachians;
   "Southeast Piedmont" puts the Piedmont there; "Gulf & Atlantic Coastal Plains" names both coasts. This is what changed rows 36-39.
3. **My reading of the geography and vegetation** (`basis = GEO`): opinion, not checked against any floristic source.
4. **Owner-dependent** (`basis = OWNER`): the evidence does not decide; it is the owner's rule that must.

Share of land area by basis: NAME 46.8%, GEO 25.4%, **OWNER 27.9%**. Over a quarter of the country's area is decided by something I could not recover.

## 3. States (no unit is silently assigned)

| State | Meaning | Units | Land |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assigned` | one region; the name or plain geography decides it | 36 | 42.4% |
| `assigned-near` | one region carried, and a named alternative is a defensible reading of where a line runs | 33 | 36.3% |
| `uncertain` | the evidence does not decide between the listed regions; **`primary_region` is only a carried best guess so a resolver has an answer** | 16 | 21.3% |

The resolver returns the state and the alternatives with every answer (`unitState`, `unitAlternatives`); a client must show an `uncertain` answer as a
question, not a fact. Of v1's 51 straddling units: 2 are now plain `assigned` (21 Southern Rockies, 38 Boston Mountains), 33 `assigned-near`, 16 `uncertain`.
No v1 non-straddler changed state.

**Subdivision.** The EPA Level IV file (`us_eco_l4`, 5,896 polygons, 968 units; downloaded, hash recorded) would let a unit be split along **official**
sub-boundaries, and the resolver format could carry that. I did **not** split any unit: the Level IV names (for example Colorado Plateaus: "Semiarid Benchlands
and Canyonlands", "Arid Canyonlands") are not decisive between two of the owner's regions, and assigning 968 sub-units by name would be inventing a
boundary rule. `uncertain` is the honest state until the owner states one.

## 4. What changed from v1

Only four primaries changed, all because the assumed name "Appalachian" became the recovered "Appalachian-Ozark Highlands": units 36 Ouachita, 37 Arkansas
Valley, 38 Boston Mountains, 39 Ozark Highlands, MW to **AP**. **The other 81 primaries are unchanged.** That is partly an honest result (the
assumed names were close for most of the map) and partly a limit of this review (the same reviewer, so the same blind spots). What changed far more is the
**state**: v1 called 34 units low, 40 medium and 11 high uncertainty by a single column; v2 says why, and 16 units (21.3% of land) are now
explicitly `uncertain`, including the largest, 65 Southeastern Plains (328,885 km2).

New regional shares (primary, area-weighted): GP 23.4%, RM 16.8%, MW 11.3%, SE 11.3%, SW 10.8%, AP 7.6%, PC 6.3%, GC 5.6%, NF 4.9%, **NE 2.0%**.
The Northeast is a sliver on this map. If "Northeast & Atlantic Woodlands" is meant to hold the Middle Atlantic coastal plain, the Pine Barrens, the Northern
Piedmont, the eastern Great Lakes lowlands and the Allegheny plateau, it is far larger; units 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 83, 84 are exactly those, and all are
`assigned-near` or `uncertain`. This is the single largest difference the owner's answer could make.

## 6. Limits

- A whole Level III unit still has one primary region; a true region line running through a unit is not representable until the owner supplies one.
- The EPA map is generalised: "boundary" positions are only as good as its mapping scale, and water bodies were merged into neighbouring polygons.
- Nothing here says where any plant occurs. Region membership of a location and of a plant are different facts (see `region-taxonomy.md` s3, item 4).
- `region-card-diagnostic.md` compares the draft card regions with GBIF points on this map; it is a consistency check labelled "draft provisional, not evidence".
